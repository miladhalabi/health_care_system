## The Prompt

**Task:** Act as a Senior Software Architect and Health-Tech Consultant. Create a professional, detailed, and visually engaging presentation outline for a graduation project titled **"NHR Syria: National Health Record & Smart Queue Management System."**

**Project Context:**
NHR Syria is a national digital transformation initiative for the Syrian healthcare sector. It replaces fragmented paper-based systems with a centralized, real-time ecosystem connecting citizens, clinics, and pharmacies.

**Core Technical Stack:**
- **Architecture:** Monorepo (npm workspaces), Layered Backend (Node.js, Express, Prisma ORM).
- **Frontend:** React, Tailwind CSS v4, PWA (Offline support).
- **Database:** PostgreSQL (Single Source of Truth).
- **Real-Time:** WebSockets (Socket.io) for live synchronization.

**Slide-by-Slide Content (24 Slides):**
1.  **Title Slide:** Project Name, Subtitle, and Team Info.
2.  **Introduction & National Vision:** The strategic context of Syria's digital health transition.
3.  **Problem Statement 1: Data Fragmentation:** The cost and risk of paper-based systems.
4.  **Problem Statement 2: Queueing Chaos:** The impact of manual waiting on patients and staff.
5.  **Project Objectives:** General and Specific goals.
6.  **Development Methodology:** Agile/Scrum and the iterative build process.
7.  **The Proposed Solution (NHR Syria):** High-level ecosystem overview.
8.  **Comparative Analysis:** Side-by-side contrast of the Old vs. New system.
9.  **Stakeholder 1: The Citizen Journey:** Registration, history access, and live tracking.
10. **Stakeholder 2: The Clinic/Doctor Portal:** Diagnosis flow and real-time queue control.
11. **Stakeholder 3: The Pharmacy Experience:** Digital Rx verification and dispensing.
12. **Stakeholder 4: National Administration:** Dashboard, stats, and oversight.
13. **Technical Stack Overview:** React, Node.js, PostgreSQL, Socket.io.
14. **System Architecture Deep-Dive:** Monorepo, shared packages, and layered logic.
15. **Database Schema (ERD):** Detailed look at relationships and data integrity.
16. **The Pulse: Real-Time WebSockets:** How data flows instantly between portals.
17. **Security Layers:** Bcrypt, JWT, RBAC, and SSL/TLS.
18. **Data Privacy & Audit Logs:** Ensuring ethical handling of patient information.
19. **Testing Strategy:** Unit, Integration, and User Acceptance testing.
20. **Performance Benchmarks:** Lighthouse scores, latency stats, and PWA efficiency.
21. **Implementation Results:** Summary of successful deployments and UI previews.
22. **Future Enhancements:** AI Analytics, Telemedicine, and Lab integration.
23. **Sustainability & Scaling:** How the project grows to a national level.
24. **Conclusion & Q&A:** Closing remarks and acknowledgments.

**Tone and Style:**
- **Tone:** Professional, authoritative, and innovative.
- **Language:** Use formal Arabic for key headings and messages, with English for technical terms.
- **Visuals:** Suggest specific diagrams for each slide (e.g., "Architecture Diagram," "User Journey Flowchart," "Security Layers Graphic").

**Visual Style & Aesthetic Guidelines:**
- **Color Palette:**
    - *Primary:* Deep Medical Blue (`#0056b3`) or Cyan (`#00a8cc`) representing trust and technology.
    - *Secondary:* Clean White (`#ffffff`) and Soft Light Grey (`#f8f9fa`) for a modern, clinical feel.
    - *Accent:* Success Green (`#28a745`) for health/completed tasks and Amber (`#ffc107`) for warnings/live status.
- **Typography:**
    - *English:* Modern Sans-Serif fonts like **Inter**, **Roboto**, or **Open Sans**.
    - *Arabic:* High-readability modern fonts like **Cairo**, **Tajawal**, or **IBM Plex Sans Arabic**.
- **Imagery & Icons:**
    - Use minimalist line icons for healthcare (stethoscopes, prescriptions) and technology (databases, cloud, sync).
    - Include high-quality mockups of the **PWA interface** on mobile and desktop devices.
    - Use clean, simple charts (Pie/Bar) for the "Comparative Analysis" and "Testing Results" slides.
- **Layout:**
    - Minimalist design with generous white space.
    - Consistency in "Right-to-Left" (RTL) alignment for Arabic slides and "Left-to-Right" (LTR) for English slides.
    - Use "Dark Mode" screenshots for technical slides to emphasize the modern tech stack.

**Alternative Presentation Themes:**
1.  **The "Tech-Focused" Dark Theme:**
    - *Background:* Dark Navy or Slate (`#0f172a`).
    - *Accents:* Neon Cyan and Electric Blue.
    - *Vibe:* Cutting-edge, software-centric, perfect for a deep-dive into the stack.
2.  **The "National/Formal" Theme:**
    - *Background:* Pure White or very light Cream.
    - *Accents:* Royal Blue and Gold or Silver.
    - *Vibe:* Institutional, stable, and official. Suitable for government or university committee presentations.
3.  **The "Clean/Minimalist" Theme:**
    - *Background:* Soft Off-White (`#f3f4f6`).
    - *Accents:* Sage Green or Soft Teal.
    - *Vibe:* Patient-centric, approachable, and calm. Emphasizes ease of use and healthcare quality.
